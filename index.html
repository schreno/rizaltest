30 December 1896

Rizal’s execution at Bagumbayan, now known as Rizal Park in Manila. He was only 35 years old.

The day before, he told his sister Trinidad about his last poem, hidden inside a small alcohol stove: “Mi Último Adiós” (“My Last Farewell”), in which he expressed his love for the Philippines and his willingness to sacrifice his life for his country.
